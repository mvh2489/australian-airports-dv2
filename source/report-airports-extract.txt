L120@P6: There were 65.32 million passengers carried on Australian domestic commercial aviation
L121@P6: (including charter operations) in the year ending June 2026, an increase of 2.4% compared

L123@P6: June 2019.
L124@P6: 60.75 million passengers were carried on RPT flights in the year ending June 2026, an
L125@P6: increase of 2.2% compared with the year ending June 2025.
L126@P6: In the year ending June 2026 there were 602.9 thousand RPT aircraft trips compared to
L127@P6: 601.0 thousand in the previous year, an increase of 0.3%.
L128@P6: RPT revenue passenger kilometres (RPKs) performed were 73.26 billion, an increase of
L129@P6: 2.3% compared with the year ending June 2025.
L130@P6: Capacity, measured by available seat kilometres (ASKs), increased by 3.2% compared with
L131@P6: the year ending June 2025, to a total of 89.08 billion.
L132@P6: The industry wide load factor (RPKs/ASKs) decreased from 82.9% in the year ending June
L133@P6: 2025 to 82.2% in the year ending June 2026. The highest load factor (91.8%) was recorded
L134@P6: on the Adelaide – Gold Coast route while the lowest load factor (53.7%) was recorded on
L135@P6: the Geraldton – Perth route.
L136@P6: For the year ending June 2026, Melbourne – Sydney remained Australia’s busiest RPT
L137@P6: route with 8.08 million passengers, an increase of 0.3% compared with the previous
L138@P6: calendar year. It was followed by Brisbane – Sydney with 4.43 million passengers
L139@P6: (up 1.6%) and Brisbane – Melbourne with 3.70 million passengers (up 4.0%).
L140@P6: In the year ending June 2026, Sydney was Australia’s busiest domestic airport with 25.50
L141@P6: million passenger movements, up 1.2% compared with the year ending June 2025, followed
L142@P6: by Melbourne with 24.61 million passenger movements (up 2.1%) and Brisbane with
L143@P6: 17.74 million passenger movements (up 2.5%).
L144@P6: Total cargo movements at Australian airports on domestic RPT flights were 321.9 thousand
L145@P6: tonnes in the year ending June 2026, an increase of 0.3% compared to the year ending
L146@P6: June 2025.

L1192@P30: DEFINITIONS
L1193@P30: Aircraft Trips The number of flight stages. A return flight counts as two
L1194@P30: aircraft trips.
L1195@P30: Airport Movements Airport movements (passenger and cargo) are measured
L1196@P30: once on arrival and once on departure. Each domestic trip
L1197@P30: generates two movements at airports. For example, a trip
L1198@P30: from Melbourne to Sydney will be counted twice, as a
L1199@P30: departure at Melbourne and as a arrival at Sydney. At the
L1200@P30: national level, the sum of all domestic airport traffic will
L1201@P30: therefore be equal to twice the number of trips undertaken.
L1202@P30: Available Seat Kilometres
L1203@P30: (ASKs)
L1204@P30: Calculated by multiplying the number of seats available on
L1205@P30: each flight stage, by the distance in kilometres between the
L1206@P30: ports. The distances used are Great Circle Distances.
L1207@P30: City-Pair The ports shown make up the city-pair route. Passenger
L1208@P30: movements shown for a city pair reflect total traffic in both
L1209@P30: directions.
L1210@P30: Flight Stage The operation of an aircraft from take-off to landing.
L1211@P30: Load Factor The total revenue passenger kilometres performed as a
L1212@P30: percentage of the total available seat kilometres.
L1213@P30: Passengers Carried Revenue passengers carried.
L1214@P30: Regular Public Transport
L1215@P30: Services (RPT)
L1216@P30: All air service operations in which aircraft are available for
L1217@P30: the transport of members of the public and are conducted in
L1218@P30: accordance with fixed schedules. It does not include charter
L1219@P30: or other non-scheduled operations.
L1220@P30: Revenue Passengers All passengers paying any fare. Frequent flyer redemption
L1221@P30: travellers are regarded as revenue passengers.
L1222@P30: Revenue Passenger
L1223@P30: Kilometres (RPKs)
L1224@P30: Calculated by multiplying the number of revenue
L1225@P30: passengers travelling on each flight stage, by the distance in
L1226@P30: kilometres between the ports. The distances used are Great
L1227@P30: Circle Distances.
L1228@P30: Traffic on board by stages The total of all traffic (revenue passengers) on each flight
L1229@P30: stage between two directly connected airports.
